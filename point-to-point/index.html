The place is made on this device from open map data. An address you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's search (Nominatim) to find where it is; numbers (latitude, longitude) are sent to nobody. The map's tiles come from Overture Maps and the ground from the USGS, both as public files on Amazon S3. Outside the United States no ground heights are to be had, and the ground is level.
